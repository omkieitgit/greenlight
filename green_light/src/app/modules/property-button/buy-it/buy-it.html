NOTE:

As a Buyer, you take a priority of being able to control the deal and get it. You assume the risk of loss on the deal when you take it down. You are the one who manages the purchase, financing, funding, and renovations. As a Buyer, any money you bring to the table is your skin in the deal. The Estates will manage any decisions on litigation. The Estates will manage Resale of the Property by working directly with the Agent/Broker. The Estates will manage pricing, billing, and listing of the Air BNB and communications with the guest prior to booking. Anyone can bid on the property at foreclosure sale.

If you are Sub To Negotiator, you are required attend the majority of the Sub To Meetups. Also you need be able to show that you can go deep on the way you work the deals. You need to be able to show that you can manage the deals you hit buy it on.

As a Lender, you can hit buy it if you may be willing to lend on the property. The Buyer will contact you if they need you to fund the deal. There is no priority for Lenders so, it is strongly recommended that you get to know the Buyers, that you can build strong relationships with.
