1. You contact the Trustee and request an extension to close on the property of 30 days when it is around 9 days after the sale has confirmed.

2. Then you should request a confirmation of bid letter and get it to the closing attorney so that they can get the necessary documentation to close.
When you bid at the steps you pay the trustee DIRECTLY. If the bid gets upset. Then the trustee will send your deposit back to you at the address that was used on the bid sheet. If you bid at the steps and then you decide to upset the bid latter. If you time it right it may look like this. You bid at the steps. Time tolls. Then someone upsets and the 10 days starts over. The minute the upset happens then the trustee needs to send your deposit back. If you reach out or have Jeya reach out, then you can push them to get the deposit back If you get the deposit back before the 10 days is up then you can use that deposit money to bid again. If someone else upsets and their is margins then it strarts the 10 days over again. This give you even more time to get your deposit back. Once you have upset the bid. The money goes to the county. If you want to upset again, then you will have to pay the difference of that upset bid to the county A few counties keep your money and send it back to you at which you would need to tender another upset bid. Cumberland County is the exception. Each time you bid at the county. If someone upsets you they send your deposit back. In which case you have to take the full amount to Cumberland to bid. Also, once your money is placed at the county It is not refunded until you close on the property. If you default it can be potentially forfeited depending on what it goes back to sale for and bids up to. When you close on the property you must pay the full amount of bid to the trustee. And then, the trustee will send a Final Report of Sale to the County. You must get a hold of the Trustee administrator that you got your extension from to push them to file this document. This document NOT BEING FILED CAN HOLD UP YOUR DEPOSIT CAN HOLD UP THE EVICTION PROCESS. You can have Jeya call daily I would find out who your administrator is and push them daily to get the Final Report of Sale Filed ASAP This can create major heart burn, slow your whole project up and it can slow up your deposit just the same.
Then all bid deposits even if you defaulted on the bid would need to be refunded. In this case the Trustee administrator will have to file a Dismissal or Cancellation of the foreclosure file. Once this is done then you can get your deposit back. If you do not keep on the trustee this could take months or even years to get back if you are not on it.
